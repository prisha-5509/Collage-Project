Types of Transmission Media :

🔵 Twisted Pair Cable

Uses pairs of twisted copper wires to transmit data.

⚫ Coaxial Cable

Uses a central conductor surrounded by insulation and shielding.

💡 Fibre Optic

Uses light signals to transmit data at high speed.

📶 Wireless

Uses wireless signals to transmit data without physical cables.
