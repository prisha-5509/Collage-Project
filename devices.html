Important Networking Devices :

🔀 Switch

A switch connects multiple devices in a network and sends data to the correct device.

🌐 Router

A router connects different networks and forwards data between them.

🔌 Hub

A hub connects multiple devices and sends data to all connected devices.

📶 Repeater

A repeater receives and retransmits signals to extend network distance.
