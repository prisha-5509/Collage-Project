Types of Network Topologies :

⭐ Star Topology

All computers are connected to a central device such as a switch.

Advantage: Easy to manage.

🚌 Bus Topology

All computers are connected to one main communication cable.

Advantage: Simple and low cost.

⭕ Ring Topology

Each computer is connected to two other computers, forming a ring.

Advantage: Organized data transmission.

🔗 Mesh Topology

Devices are connected using multiple connections.

Advantage: High reliability.
